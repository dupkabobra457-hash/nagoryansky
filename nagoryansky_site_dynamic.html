Who I Am

The Comeback Story

VLADIMIR NAGORYANSKY · BLUE BELT · 69 KG

7 лет бокса — ни одной значимой победы. В 15 лет всё изменилось: на пляже в Турции я впервые попробовал бразильское джиу-джитсу. Этот момент определил мою судьбу.

«ЧМ ACBJJ 2022 — ноль побед. Я потерял сознание прямо на татами. Тренер сказал: "Тебе в женскую категорию". Я не сдался.»

Сегодня я #1 в Северной Америке и #2 в мировом рейтинге AJP (вне весовых категорий). 29 мая 2026 — чемпионат мира. Я иду за золотом.

Journey

The Path to Glory

2021
🥋 First Steps in BJJ
After 7 years of boxing without significant wins, discovered Brazilian Jiu-Jitsu on a beach in Turkey. Fell in love instantly. Started training relentlessly.
2021–2022
🏆 First Tournament Victories
Quick rise through local and regional competitions. Won first medals at Russian tournaments. Discovered natural talent for competitive grappling.
2022
💔 ACBJJ World Championship — 0 Wins
Lost consciousness on the mats. Zero victories. Coach said: "You belong in the women's division." This became the fuel for everything that followed. Refused to quit.
2023
🔥 The Comeback Begins
Rebuilt technique from scratch. Won Grand Slam Moscow Gold. Started international campaign: Qatar, Switzerland, Spain — gold after gold. Earned nickname «Baseball Bat».
2024–2025
🌍 International Dominance
10+ gold medals across AJP tour. #1 North America ranking. #2 in the world (all weight classes). Victories in Germany, Latvia, Italy, Bosnia, Austria. Became an international force.
2026
🏆 WORLD CHAMPIONSHIP · MAY 29
The ultimate goal. Everything has led to this moment. Training harder than ever. The world will witness the full power of the Baseball Bat. Gold is the only option.
